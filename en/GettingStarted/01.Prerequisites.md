# Prerequisites

Before running the scenarios, ensure core configuration and common code lists are in place. Complete these steps to prepare the system for first use.

## Prerequisites steps

### 1. Create essential Common code lists

These shared code lists are required by **[Sales](../Sales/Domain/SalesDomain.md)**, **[Supply](../Supply/Domain/SupplyDomain.md)**, **[Logistics](../Logistics/Domain/LogisticsDomain.md)**, **[Production](../Production/Domain/ProductionDomain.md)**, and **[System](../System/Settings/Configuration.md)**:

- **[Countries](../Common/CodeLists/Countries.md)** — list of countries used for addresses, localization, and document formatting
- **[Currencies](../Common/CodeLists/Currencies.md)** — currencies available for pricing, documents, and financial reports
- **[Tax rates](../Common/CodeLists/TaxRates.md)** — VAT and other tax definitions applied on sales and supply documents
- **[Measure units](../Common/CodeLists/MeasureUnits.md)** — base units (pcs, kg, m, etc.) used for quantities and calculations

Why this matters: Domains and System settings depend on these values for correct behavior.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> Common code lists must be created before selecting them in System configuration or domain documents. See **[Common domain](../Common/Domain/CommonDomain.md)** for details.

### 2. Configure System settings

Set the organization and global defaults used across all modules:
- **[System configuration](../System/Settings/Configuration.md)** — organization identity, legal data, footer; default country and currency
  - Configure Organization (identity, legal details, footer)
  - Set Common Types (default Country, default Currency)

Why this matters: The organization profile, country, and currency are reused by all domains and documents.

### 3. Configure payment settings (Sales)

If your scenarios include Sales documents, configure payment data:
- **[Payment methods](../Sales/CodeLists/PaymentMethods.md)** — supported customer payment types (bank transfer, card, cash, etc.)
- **[Organization bank accounts](../Sales/CodeLists/OrganizationBankAccounts.md)** — company bank accounts shown on invoices and used for payments

Why this matters: Invoices and prepayments require valid payment definitions.

### 4. Add business partners (Business directory)

Add business partners (customers, vendors, cooperators) to use them across procurement, sales, and logistics:
- **[Business directory](../Common/CodeLists/BusinessDirectory.md)** — centralized list of customers, suppliers, and partner entities

Why this matters: Documents need partner data (customer, supplier) to be created.

> [!TIP]
> Prepare a basic list of customers and suppliers (name, tax id, address) before starting the Business directory.

### 5. Materials and Assets

Add materials and assets that will be used in documents and stock operations:
- **[Materials](../Assets/Domain/Materials.md)** — overview of material types (like [finished products](../Assets/CodeLists/Products.md) or [raw materials](../Assets/CodeLists/RawMaterials.md)) and their usage.
- **[Assets](../Assets/CodeLists/Assets.md)** — catalog of goods and services with pricing and tax info.

Why this matters: Sales and production documents require defined items and resources.

### 6. Set up warehouses and locations (if applicable)

For logistics or production scenarios involving stock:
- **[Warehouses](../Logistics/CodeLists/Warehouses.md)** — storage sites where stock is held
- **[Locations](../Logistics/CodeLists/Locations.md)** — bin/shelf locations inside warehouses for precise placement

Why this matters: Stock operations require defined storage sites and locations.

### 7. Verify via domain entry points

Use domain overviews to confirm everything resolves and loads:
- **[Sales domain](../Sales/Domain/SalesDomain.md)** — documents, views, and management for the sales area
- **[Supply domain](../Supply/Domain/SupplyDomain.md)** — procurement documents, supplier catalogs, and related views
- **[Logistics domain](../Logistics/Domain/LogisticsDomain.md)** — warehouse documents, stock views, and movement reports
- **[Production domain](../Production/Domain/ProductionDomain.md)** — execution, production orders, quality, and analytics
- **[Assets domain](../Assets/Domain/AssetsDomain.md)** — materials, assets catalogs, and related management

Why this matters: If domains open without errors and show expected lists, configuration is consistent.
