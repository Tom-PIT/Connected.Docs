# Restock or produce

This tutorial explains, in simple terms, what to do when you get a sales order and don’t have enough stock before delivery.

- You can buy more (resupply) — Order the missing goods from a supplier. Example: You sell pine tables and have 2 in stock but need 10. Create a supply order for 8 finished tables (or for wood, legs, screws if you assemble in‑house).
- Or you can produce more (make) — Build the missing goods with your own process. Example: You manufacture pine tables from wood and hardware. Create a production order for 8 tables and consume the needed materials.

> [!NOTE]
> Which option applies depends on your business model:
> - Trading company (buy/sell finished goods) → usually Buy
> - Manufacturer (you make the goods) → usually Produce (and buy raw materials if missing)
> - Services/software → usually neither (no stock to move)

> [!TIP]
> Before you start: ensure **[Processes](../Production/CodeLists/Processes.md)**, **[Inputs](../Production/CodeLists/Inputs.md)**, and **[Outputs](../Production/CodeLists/Outputs.md)** are defined.

## End-to-end flow

This section shows the full resupply/produce flow for a production company; your flow may differ based on business model and use case.

### Detect shortage
Identify missing items from the **[Sales order](../Sales/Documents/SalesOrders.md)** or **[Delivery note](../Sales/Documents/DeliveryNotes.md)** (items in red/insufficient), and use **[Requirements](../Production/Documents/Requirements.md)** to see materials short for planned operations.

### Resupply (buy)
1. (Optional) Send an **[Inquiry](../Supply/Documents/Inquiries.md)** to request quotes and lead times.
2. Create a **[Supply order](../Supply/Documents/SupplyOrders.md)** for the missing items.
3. Receive the products into stock:
   - Use **[Receives](../Logistics/Documents/Receives.md)** (or linked receive from the supply order)
   - Verify locations and quantities in **[Stock](../Logistics/Documents/Stock.md)**

### Produce (make)

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
>Before creating a production order, ensure your process and operations are defined in **[Processes](../Production/CodeLists/Processes.md)**.

1. Create a **[Production order](../Production/Documents/ProductionOrders.md)** (if manufacturing applies):
   - Select product, process, quantity, due date, and organization unit
   - When ready, activate the order; it will appear in **[Execution](../Production/Documents/Execution.md)** for recording work
2. Use **[Execution](../Production/Documents/Execution.md)** to enter produced items and specify: 
   - **[Consumed](../Production/Documents/Consumed.md)** — materials used during production
   - **[Effort](../Production/Documents/Effort.md)** — labor time spent
   - **[Loss](../Production/CodeLists/LossClassificationTags.md)** — quantity scrapped and the reason (if any)
   - **[Downtime](../Production/Documents/Downtime.md)** — equipment stops and duration (if any)

> [!NOTE]
> Use **[Requirements](../Production/Documents/Requirements.md)** to resolve missing materials for planned production operations; create supply orders if inputs are short.
