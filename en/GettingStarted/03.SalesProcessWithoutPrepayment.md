# Sales process (without prepayment)

This guide walks through a typical sales process without using prepayments. Use links for details; do not duplicate configuration here.

## Overview

Flow: Offer → Sales order → Delivery note/Issues → Issued invoice → Payments

![Sales flow without prepayment](../GettingStarted/Assets/SalesFlowWithoutPrepayment.svg "Sales flow without prepayment")

> [!NOTE]
> - In some use cases the process starts directly with a **Sales order**. The **Offer** step is optional.
> - Inventory movement occurs at **Issue**. 
> - **Issued invoices** record revenue and payments.


## Steps

### 1. Create and publish an Offer (optional)

- Use the **[action button](../Common/UI/ActionButton.md)** to create a draft **[Offer](../Sales/Documents/Offers.md)**
- Fill Customer, validity, and add items
- Publish to confirm the commercial terms

### 2. Create a Sales order

- From the committed Offer, use Linked documents → + **[Sales order](../Sales/Documents/SalesOrders.md)**
- Confirm quantities and delivery details

### 2a. Restock or produce (if needed)

If items are unavailable, see **[Restock or produce](06.RestockOrProduce.md)** for options to procure or manufacture before delivery.

### 3. Deliver goods

Follow these operational steps to move stock out of the warehouse:

1. From the Sales order, open Linked documents → + **[Delivery note](../Sales/Documents/DeliveryNotes.md)**.  
   Verify customer, delivery address, and add the items to be shipped.
2. Click **Publish** on the Delivery note.  
   This confirms the shipment document (no stock move yet).
3. In the Delivery note, open Linked documents → + **[Issue](../Logistics/Documents/Issues.md)**.  
   Select the warehouse and location(s), confirm quantities to pick.
4. Click **Publish** on the Issue.  
   This moves stock and updates inventory balances.
5. (Optional) Verify the change in **[Stock view by location](../Logistics/Views/StockViewByLocation.md)**.

### 4. Issue the final invoice and record payments

- From Delivery note or Sales order, create an **[Issued invoice](../Sales/Documents/IssuedInvoices.md)**
- Publish and record incoming payments (partial or full)

> [!TIP]
> - Use **Linked documents** to step through each stage and confirm auto-filled data (customer, items).
> - Open **[Company cards](../Sales/Views/CompanyCards.md)** to review customer history, orders, and balances.
