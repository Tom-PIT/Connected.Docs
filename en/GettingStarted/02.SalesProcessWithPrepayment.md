# Sales process (with prepayment)

This guide walks through a typical sales process including a prepayment step. Use links for details; do not duplicate configuration here.

## Overview

Flow: Offer → Proforma invoice → Prepayment → Sales order → Delivery note/Issues → Issued invoice → Payments

![Sales flow with prepayment](../GettingStarted/Assets/SalesFlowWithPrepayment.svg "Sales flow with prepayment")

> [!NOTE]
> Inventory movement occurs at **Issue**. **Prepayment** and **Issued invoice** record financial transactions.

## Steps

### 1. Create and publish a sales offer

- Go to **Sales / Documents / Offers**
- Use the [**action button**](../Common/UI/ActionButton.md) to create a draft [offer](../Sales/Documents/Offers.md)
- Fill Customer, validity, and add items
- Click **Publish** to confirm the commercial terms

### 2. Create a proforma invoice

- From the committed offer, use **Linked documents → [**+ Proforma invoice**](../Sales/Documents/ProformaInvoices.md)**
- Adjust references, bank account, and validity
- Click **Publish**

### 3. Record a prepayment (partial or full)

- From the committed proforma invoice, use **Linked documents → [+ Prepayment](../Sales/Documents/Prepayments.md)**
- Fill due date, reference type/number, org bank account, and payment method. Adjust product quantities according to how much the customer has agreed to pay in advance.
- Click **Publish** to commit the received advance

### 4. Create a sales order

- Go back to the previously committed offer, and use **Linked documents → [+ Sales order](../Sales/Documents/SalesOrders.md)**
- Confirm quantities and delivery details and click **Publish**

### 4a. Restock or produce (if needed)

If items are unavailable, see **[Restock or produce](06.RestockOrProduce.md)** for options to procure or manufacture before delivery.

### 5. Deliver goods

Follow these operational steps to move stock out of the warehouse:

1. From the sales order, open **Linked documents → [+ Delivery note](../Sales/Documents/DeliveryNotes.md)**.  
   Verify customer, delivery address, and add the items to be shipped.
2. In the delivery note, open **Linked documents → [+ Full issue](../Logistics/Documents/Issues.md)**.  
   Select the warehouse and location(s), confirm quantities to pick.
4. Click **Publish** to commit the issue.  
   This moves stock out of our warehouse and updates inventory balances.
5. (Optional) Verify the change in **[Stock view by location](../Logistics/Views/StockViewByLocation.md)**.

### 6. Issue the final invoice and apply prepayment

- From the delivery note or the sales order, open **Linked documents → [+ Issued invoice](../Sales/Documents/IssuedInvoices.md)**
- Apply the previously recorded prepayment via **Linked documents → [Prepayment](../Sales/Documents/Prepayments.md)** to reduce the due amount
- Click **Publish** and record remaining payments via the **Payment** button, if needed.

> [!TIP]
> - Use **Linked documents** to step through each stage and confirm auto-filled data (customer, items).
> - Open **[Company cards](../Sales/Views/CompanyCards.md)** to review customer history, orders, and balances.
