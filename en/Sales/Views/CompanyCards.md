# Company cards

The **Company cards** view provides an overview of the financial balance between your company and each customer. It helps you quickly identify **how much each customer owes you (debit)** or **how much you owe them (credit)** across all issued invoices.

This screen does **not** create or modify documents. It is an analytical view that consolidates data from **Issued invoices**. It is especially useful for **accounting**, **credit control**, and **cash-flow monitoring**.


To access this view, go to **Sales / Views / Company cards** in the [navigation](../../Common/UI/Navigation.md).

![Company cards overview](../Assets/CompanyCardsList.png)

## Company cards list

Each row represents a **customer** and their **current balance**, derived from their issued invoices:

- **Debit balance** → The customer owes money to your company  
- **Credit balance** → Your company owes money to the customer (e.g., overpayments, corrections)  

Clicking a row opens the related document (e.g., an **issued invoice**), allowing you to inspect or record payments.

## Filters

The left sidebar allows you to refine the results:

- **Created date** – Filters invoices by creation date  
- **Due date** – Filters invoices by due date  
- **Company card type**  
  - *All*  
  - *Debit*  
  - *Credit*  
- **Company** – Select a specific customer  

## Purpose

This view helps answer high-level questions such as:

- Which customers still owe money? 
- What is the total value of unpaid invoices per customer?
- Are there customers with a credit balance?
