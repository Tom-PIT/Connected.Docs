# Work items costs

A compact view of unit cost per production order. Compare trends and open a quick breakdown.

- Access: Resources / Work items costs (see navigation)
- Purpose: Spot cost regressions and investigate fastest

## List (summary)
- Production order, Product, Good qty
- Unit cost, Total cost
- Trend arrow vs previous order (same product/process)
- Date, Organization unit (if set)

## Detail (on click)
- Breakdown: Materials, Effort, Expenses
- Charts: Cost trend, Distribution, Performance (best/worst)

> [!TIP]
> Cross-check closed orders in **[Production orders](../../Production/Documents/ProductionOrders.md)**.
