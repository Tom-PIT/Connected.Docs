# Human resources

Human resources define the **people and roles** involved in operations. You can assign specific workers or job positions to operations to plan and record effort.

To access this page, open a process version from **Production / Management / [Processes](Processes.md)**, click **[Operations](Operations.md)**, then select **Human resources** for the chosen operation.

![Human resources button](../Assets/HumanResourcesButton.png "Human resources button")

> [!TIP]
> For a full demonstration, see the **[Human and Non-human resources](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iq7fQiPh_i4)** video tutorial.

## Schema

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Entity** | Assign a **Human resource** or a **Job position**. |
| **Quantity** | Number of people required. |
| **Time** | Expected time per person (e.g., minutes per batch/unit). |
| **Ordinal** | Display order. |
| **Tags** | Optional tags for grouping or filtering. |

## List view

Shows all human resources linked to the selected operation.

## Creating a new human resource entry

1. Click the **action button** and choose **New**.
2. Fill in the required fields.
3. Click **Add**.

## Editing

1. Click the entry in the list.
2. Adjust fields.
3. Click **Save**.

## Deletion

Click **Delete** on the Edit page.
