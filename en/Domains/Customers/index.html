    Customers¶

    The Customers domain provides tools for managing customer interactions, support requests, and service-related communication. It enables organizations to track incoming requests, resolve issues, monitor service levels, and maintain structured customer-related data.

    Where the Sales domain focuses on commercial transactions, the Customers domain focuses on post-sale communication, issue resolution, and customer assistance.

    To access this domain, navigate to Customers from the main sitemap.

    Note

    The available domains and features depend on the company configuration and enabled modules.

    What is included in the Customers domain?¶

    The domain is organized into the following functional areas:

    • Visitors – management of external users and customer contacts
    • Tickets – handling of support requests and service issues
    • Views – analytical and summary views related to customer support
    • Management – configuration and master data for customer support processes

    Visitors¶

    The Visitors section focuses on customers and partners physical visits to company locations.

    The Visitors management screen records physical visits to company locations with visitor details, date/time, status (Announced, On location, Completed, Cancelled), and target location.

    Tickets¶

    The Tickets section contains all support-related records used to track customer requests and service issues.

    It includes screens for:

    • Tickets – active and open support requests
    • Resolved tickets – completed and closed support cases
    • SLA tickets – tickets monitored against defined service level agreements

    These screens represent the core operational area of the Customers domain, allowing support agents to follow tickets from creation through resolution while maintaining visibility of response and resolution times.

    Views¶

    The Views section provides read-only screens that present customer-related information in a structured and aggregated form.

    Available views include:

    • Company cards – overview of customers and their related activity across the system, including support-related information

    Views do not create or modify data. They are intended for analysis, navigation, and decision support.

    Management¶

    The Management section contains configuration screens and code lists required to support customer service processes.

    Available configuration screens include:

    • Notification settings – personalization of ticket-related notifications per desk
    • Desks – definition of support desks and service channels
    • Business directory – shared directory of companies and contacts used across domains
    • Institutional sectors – classification of customers by sector
    • Job titles – role definitions associated with customer contacts and users

    These elements define how customer support is structured and how users interact with tickets and notifications.

    Tip

    See all management entries in the Management Index.

    Customers and other domains¶

    The Customers domain integrates with several other domains:

    Area Interaction
    Sales Customer and company data shared with commercial processes
    Projects Tickets may relate to ongoing customer projects
    Maintenance Support tickets can be linked to maintenance orders or issues
    Resources Support workload and responsibility distribution
    Knowledge Documentation and knowledge base entries supporting issue resolution
