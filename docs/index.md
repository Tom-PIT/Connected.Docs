# Connected Documentation

Tom PIT.Connected is a global digital transformation platform that covers the full spectrum of business operations across domains such as Sales, Supply, Logistics, Production, Assets, Projects, and more. As a comprehensive ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) platform, Tom PIT.Connected integrates and manages key organizational processes within a single unified system.
